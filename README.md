# JobSuit

### Understand your fit. Improve your CV. Apply with confidence.

JobSuit is a web-based job compatibility analysis and CV improvement platform designed to help job seekers understand how well their qualifications align with specific job opportunities.

Users can upload their CVs, maintain their professional profiles, and provide job opportunities through screenshots, URLs, or manual entry. The system analyses the available job requirements against the user's skills, education, work experience, and projects.

Rather than simply assigning a compatibility score, JobSuit identifies matching qualifications, potential skill gaps, and opportunities to improve how existing experience is presented in a CV.

## Key Features

- **CV Upload and Analysis** – Upload PDF or DOCX CVs and extract relevant information.
- **Professional Profile Management** – Maintain skills, education, work experience, and projects.
- **Flexible Job Input** – Provide job opportunities through screenshots, accessible URLs, or manual entry.
- **Job Compatibility Analysis** – Compare professional qualifications against job requirements.
- **Skill Gap Identification** – Identify matched, partially matched, and not-evidenced requirements.
- **CV Improvement Recommendations** – Receive suggestions based on genuine qualifications and experience.
- **Job-Specific CV Customisation** – Prepare tailored CV versions using user-approved information.
- **Application Tracking** – Organise job applications and monitor their progress.

## Core Principles

- Never invent or fabricate user qualifications or experience.
- Distinguish missing CV information from an actual lack of skills.
- Provide understandable explanations for compatibility results.
- Keep users in control of their personal information and CV content.
- Treat compatibility scores as an indication of documented alignment, not a prediction of hiring success.
